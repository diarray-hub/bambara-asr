# An bɛ kalan paper: source reconciliation

This is a correction log for `an-be-kalan-bench/docs/632_Building_an_ASR_Solution_f_paper.pdf`, checked against the June 2026 technical report, the experiment YAML and training code, the published dataset and model cards, and released model configurations. "Verify" entries need training logs, raw metadata, or author confirmation before a categorical paper correction.

| Paper claim or ambiguity | Evidence | Proposed treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| QuartzNet used over 100 maximum epochs; Soloni's cap is omitted. | Both Exp3 YAML files specify `training.epochs: 50`; the technical report states 50 for every experiment and lists observed stopping epochs. | Correct the maximum to 50 for both families; distinguish cap from actual stop epoch. |
| Evaluation is described as an isolated/held-out test and as having no reader overlap. | Both Exp3 YAML files point `valid` and `test` to `test-manifest.jsonl`; `train.py` monitors `val_wer` for early stopping and checkpoint selection. Validation uses `max_duration: 10` s for Soloni and 15 s for QuartzNet, whereas test uses 30 s. Thus validation is a duration-filtered portion of the test manifest, as confirmed by the author. The dataset card also allows rare train/test speaker overlap because IDs are inconsistent. | State that book content is separated, but the benchmark was reused for checkpoint selection. Avoid "untouched held-out test" or categorical speaker-disjoint claims. Report new intervals as conditional/descriptive and identify this limitation. |
| Exp3/4 training volume is 47.2 h in Table I, while model cards and the report say 45.6 h total. | Dataset card gives main train 1.62 h and duplicate train 44.02 h, summing to about 45.64 h. The technical report table's `Main + Dup (45.6h)` is consistent with that sum; some prose calls the duplicate addition 45.6 h. | Use about 45.6 h total (1.62 + 44.02) and about 44 h added. Verify against the actual training manifests if exact time is important. |
| The collection description says 22 textbooks while the benchmark has 11 further test books. | Dataset card lists 22 main-train books and 11 distinct main-test books. | Clarify whether "22" refers to training books or the collection campaign; avoid implying the 11 test books are among the 22. |
| The released test split contains 11 books. | Direct inspection of `main/test` at revision `95cf3103994ac13c13e086518c320a614cf75085` finds **17 distinct `BookTitle` values**, including six titles absent from the card's test inventory. They are substantive titles, not spelling variants of the listed 11. | Audit the card and paper's book count. Use the 17 observed title clusters for current uncertainty analysis unless an authoritative grouping table identifies merged works. |
| Data collection participants are described as ages 9–18; the dataset card gives 8–19 across the combined dataset. | The two sources may describe different populations or metadata versions. | Verify the collection cohort and the released dataset separately; qualify the reported range. |
| The dataset card says the combined age range starts at 8. | Direct inspection of the released test split finds two utterances with `speakerAge=5`. | Audit those two records and correct the card or metadata. They currently fall in the under-10 cohort. |
| The paper's cohort WER discussion omits missing demographics and the remaining age group. | The dataset card lists 724 test utterances and 38 with missing speaker metadata; paper/report cite 93 under 10 and 527 aged 10–15. These counts leave 104 utterances in other/unknown ages. | Recompute counts from the pinned test revision. State the age boundaries and show the older/unknown remainder rather than implying the two cohorts cover the whole test. |
| The technical report says duration-filtered validation comprised about 85% of the benchmark test set. | At the inspected release revision, 32 of 724 recordings exceed Soloni's 10-second validation limit and 5 exceed QuartzNet's 15-second limit, giving at most 692/724 (95.6%) and 719/724 (99.3%) before any additional filtering. | Reconstruct the exact historical validation manifest and filtering code before reporting a percentage. Retain the shared-manifest limitation regardless. |
| Table I says 4/10/10 are the maximum number of randomly applied masks. | NeMo `SpecAugment` and `SpecCutout` apply the configured number of masks, with random positions and widths. Released Exp3 checkpoints have mask counts zero; Exp2/4 CLI overrides require their exact run commands or logs for complete confirmation. | Describe 4 frequency and 10 time masks for augmented Soloni runs, and 10 rectangles for augmented QuartzNet runs, subject to run-log verification. The models are not assigned all three mask types. |
| The paper cites original SpecAugment as though the training policy were identical. | Park et al. include time warping, frequency masking, and time masking. NeMo's Soloni config uses frequency/time masking without time warping; QuartzNet uses rectangular `SpecCutout`. | Name the actual variants and state that time warping was not used. |
| Tokenizer source is not documented; the older Soloni v2 card attributes its tokenizer to Kunkado, while the author says AfVoices train. | `train.py` does not replace the tokenizer for these experiments; the released Soloni checkpoint contains a 512-piece BPE tokenizer inherited from its base. The training corpus provenance is inconsistent across sources. | Use the author's AfVoices-train attribution in a draft section but mark it as requiring provenance confirmation before final paper publication. |
| Soloni is described as combining TDT and CTC yet the deployment rationale calls both paths non-autoregressive. | The released Soloni card describes TDT as auto-regressive and CTC as the non-autoregressive path. Reported 22%/8% results are labelled CTC. | Specify the CTC inference branch for the reported benchmark and on-device timing; avoid calling TDT non-autoregressive. |
| Both released models are assumed to support Monte Carlo dropout. | The released Soloni encoder config has dropout 0.1, attention dropout 0.1, and batch normalization. Every listed QuartzNet Jasper block has `dropout: 0.0`. | Activate only Soloni's nonzero dropout modules for stochastic inference; mark Monte Carlo dropout unavailable for QuartzNet. Never substitute SpecAugment or an altered dropout rate and call it the released model. |
| Large claims that duplicated speech or SpecAugment outperform/regularize other configurations are based on point estimates. | The paper itself says no significance tests were performed; the model-selection reuse further narrows interpretation. | Reassess after paired uncertainty analysis. Preserve causal language only where supported by design and uncertainty. |
